<h2>🧐 What Is Lilo?</h2>

Lilo is a friendly, compact note-taking app that lives right on your Windows desktop. Unlike big, complicated programs, Lilo feels light and fast. You write your notes in a simple format called Markdown, which just means you use plain text with a few special symbols to make things bold, italic, or organized into lists. Even if you have never heard of Markdown, you will pick it up in minutes.

But here is the magic: Lilo remembers how your notes relate to each other. When you write a note about "Coffee" and mention "Morning Routine" in another note, Lilo automatically shows you a visual map, like a family tree of your thoughts. This map is called a <em>knowledge graph</em>. It helps you see connections you might have missed, making it perfect for students, writers, researchers, or anyone who loves learning.

<h2>✨ Why You Will Love Lilo</h2>

- <strong>📦 Compact and Lightweight</strong>: Lilo is built with Rust, a programming language known for speed and efficiency. It starts instantly and uses very little memory. It will not slow down your computer.
- <strong>🔗 Built-In Knowledge Graph</strong>: Watch your ideas form a beautiful web of connections automatically. No extra setup, no cloud account. It just works.
- <strong>📝 Markdown Made Easy</strong>: Write with simple formatting that turns into clean, readable notes. Perfect for sharing or exporting.
- <strong>🏷️ Wiki-Style Links</strong>: Type something like <code>[[Important Idea]]</code> in any note, and Lilo will create a direct link to a note with that name. It is like having a mini-Wikipedia for your own brain.
- <strong>🔒 Source-Available and Private</strong>: Your notes stay on your machine. There is no cloud, no tracking, and no account required. Your data belongs to you.
- <strong>🎨 Clean and Simple Interface</strong>: No clutter. Just you, your words, and the connections between them.

<h2>❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

### <strong>Is Lilo free to use?</strong>

Yes! The application is completely free. You can download it, use it forever, and even share it with friends.

### <strong>Do I need to know how to code?</strong>

Absolutely not. If you can write an email, you can use Lilo. Markdown is just a few simple symbols. For example, putting an asterisk <code>*</code> around a word makes it italic. Putting two <code>**</code> makes it bold. And typing <code>#</code> at the start of a line makes it a heading. Lilo shows you the formatted result instantly.

### <strong>Where are my notes stored?</strong>

Lilo saves your notes locally on your computer in a dedicated folder. You can easily back them up by copying that folder to a USB drive or cloud folder like OneDrive or Dropbox. Your notes are plain text files, so they will remain readable forever.

### <strong>Can I export my notes?</strong>

Yes. Since your notes are stored in standard Markdown format, you can open them in any text editor. Lilo also gives you options to copy your formatted notes directly to the clipboard for pasting into other apps.

### <strong>What if I find a bug or have a suggestion?</strong>

Lilo improves thanks to user feedback. On the main GitHub page, you will find a tab called <strong>"Issues."</strong> You can post a report there. Be as specific as possible: what you did, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. This helps the developer fix problems quickly.

<h2>🔍 Tips for Power Users</h2>

- <strong>⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts</strong>: Learn the most common shortcuts. Press <code>Ctrl + N</code> to create a new note. Press <code>Ctrl + P</code> to quickly search through all your notes. Press <code>Ctrl + G</code> to focus on the knowledge graph view.
- <strong>🏷️ Tags</strong>: Markdown supports tags. At the top of a note, type <code>Tags: #important #work</code>. You can then click on a tag to see all notes with that tag.
- <strong>🔤 Splitting Notes</strong>: If a note gets too long, select some text, right-click, and choose <strong>"Split into New Note."</strong> Lilo will create a new note and automatically link the two together. This is a fast way to organize.
- <strong>🎨 Customization</strong>: Explore the settings menu. You can change the app theme (light or dark), adjust the font size, and decide how the knowledge graph arranges its nodes.
